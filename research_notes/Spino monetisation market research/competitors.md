## Dance learning apps (Steezy, Dance Reality, salsa/bachata-specific apps): prices and models

### Takeaway
Content-led dance apps either run subscriptions (STEEZY at roughly £60–£100/yr in the UK) or sell low-priced course/pack IAPs ($3.99–$5.99). The bachata-specific App Store apps are tiny: they have single-digit rating counts and are often white-label course apps (Passion.io). None owns partner-dance learners at scale.

### Cited Findings
- STEEZY's UK App Store IAPs are: Standard Monthly £5.99, Basic Monthly £9.99, Monthly Subscription £26.49, Standard/Basic Yearly £59.99, Standard Yearly Access £99.99, plus deprecated yearly tiers at £129.99/£154.99/£169.99. The listing has 1.5k ratings at 4.5. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/steezy-learn-how-to-dance/id1296001664)
- STEEZY US pricing is ~$19.99/month or $99.99/year for Standard, with a $9.99 Basic Monthly tier (App Pricing Lab, data to May 2026). Adapty's paywall library (updated Sep 2024) also lists Basic Yearly $59.99 and Monthly $29.99. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/steezy/), [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/apple/1296001664)
- STEEZY offers a 7-day trial according to one listing aggregator (may be dated). — [AppsHunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/1296001664/steezy/how-to-cancel-subscription)
- STEEZY revenue estimates range from ~$19M/yr (Latka, June 2024, explicitly an estimate) to ~$6.5M (Prospeo). Funding is ~$3M seed (Caplight, CB Insights). No subscriber counts are published. — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/steezy), [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/steezy-revenue), [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/steezy)
- Dance Reality is free with one-off packs (Salsa Basics $3.99, LA Style Footwork $4.99). All styles except bachata include a free step. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dance-reality/id1277367395)
- QueWedta Dancing (Passion.io white-label) charges a $5.99 Monthly Plan or a $3.99 one-time payment. It has 3 ratings and was last updated Sep 2023. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1595834211)
- Bachata Dance Academy is free and sells courses in-app (prices hidden). It has 2 ratings and is at version 59.0.0. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6475698623)
- Bachata Pro – Dance Lessons AI sits in the 1K–10K download band on Google Play. — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/dev/MaS+Dance/)

### Inferences
- STEEZY's UK listing shows a cheap entry monthly price (£5.99) next to £60–£100 annual plans. This suggests that heavy annual-plan anchoring plus price testing is standard in the category. The odd ordering (Standard Monthly £5.99 < Basic Monthly £9.99) may be a legacy or intro product ID, so treat it with caution.
- Bachata/salsa learning apps on the stores are fragmented and low-traction (version numbers like 59.0.0 and Passion.io copyright point to white-label instructor apps). No dominant partner-dance app was found.

### Gaps
- "DanceLink" and "The Dance Buddy" were not found in searches. Their existence and pricing are unverified.
- Play Store download counts were not verified for most bachata apps. I found no bachata learning app with 100K+ downloads.
- Sensor Tower/AppFigures data was not accessible.

## Video practice / loop / analysis tools used by dancers

### Takeaway
Generic sports video-analysis tools have consolidated or died: Coach's Eye was retired in 2022 and Hudl Technique was sold to OnForm in 2021. OnForm is the surviving consumer option at $9.99/mo or $99.99/yr for individuals, with a 14-day no-card trial. It is coach-centric and not built for dance or music.

### Cited Findings
- Coach's Eye was retired by TechSmith in September 2022. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/coach-s-eye/about)
- OnForm acquired the Hudl Technique app and its user base in 2021, and Technique was then discontinued. Users were migrated to OnForm. — [PRWeb](https://www.prweb.com/releases/onform-acquires-the-hudl-technique-application-from-hudl-879040023.html), [glennpaulley.ca](https://glennpaulley.ca/curling/2021/08/24/moving-to-onform-from-hudl-technique/)
- OnForm pricing (USD ex-tax):
  - Individual Standard: $9.99/mo, $26.99/qtr, $99.99/yr.
  - Individual Premium: $14.99/mo, $149.99/yr.
  - Coach Basic/Standard/Pro: $19.99/$39.99/$59.99 per month.
  - Trial: 14 days, no card. After the trial, users keep existing videos but cannot make new recordings.
  — [OnForm](https://www.onform.com/pricing)
- Dance-specific practice players are a new crop, each with features overlapping Spino:
  - Terpsi: trimming, frame-by-frame, mirror and spaced recall, all local/iCloud.
  - DanceBeat: loop, slow, mirror and compare.
  - DanceCopilot: loop and compare.
  — [Terpsi](https://mwm.ai/apps/terpsi-dance-practice-videos/6780994356), [DanceBeat](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/dancebeat/id6757979636)

### Inferences
- Loop, slow-mo, mirror and frame-scrub are now table stakes, not differentiators.
- OnForm's "keep your old videos, can't add new" post-trial rule is a useful paywall precedent for a local-video app.

### Gaps
- Dartfish pricing was not researched (it is a professional/B2B tool and less relevant).
- Generic "loop video" / slow-motion apps were not individually priced.
- BeatCounter-type tap-tempo apps were not priced.

## Online dance academies and instructor platforms

### Takeaway
Bachata/salsa online education is dominated by instructor-owned course sites, mostly Teachable plus some Thinkific, Gumroad, Udemy and Skool. They sell one-time lifetime courses (~£40 per course to $299–$499 programmes) or ~$27/mo community memberships. Some ship white-label apps (Passion.io; Incognito Dance delivers courses via an app).

### Cited Findings
- The Dance Dojo charges $299 one-time for its Bachata Program (122 lessons, 9 levels, lifetime) and $499 for the Salsa + Bachata bundle. It offers a 30-day money-back guarantee and claims "9000+ students in 59 countries". — [Dance Dojo](https://thedancedojo.com/product/bachata/)
- Incognito Dance (London) sells online bachata courses at £40 each, accessed via an app after enrolment. Its in-person classes cost ~£13/session. — [Incognito Dance](https://incognito-dance.teachable.com/p/home)
- A bachata Skool community charges $27/month for all courses plus weekly coaching calls. — [Skool](https://www.skool.com/how-to-social-dance-in-14-days-3376/-/members?t=online)
- Instructor schools on Teachable sell packages and "VIP/styling memberships": Junior & Carolina, Dima & Victoria (V-Method), Ronald & Alba, Roberto Delgado. — [Junior & Carolina](https://junior-carolina.teachable.com/p/bachata-vip-membership-1), [Dima & Victoria](https://dima-katz-s-school.teachable.com/), [Roberto Delgado](https://roberto-delgado-villa-s-school.teachable.com/p/get-it-all)
- Korke & Judith run a certified-instructor programme (no price found). A Gumroad course by a K&J-trained instructor costs $47. — [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/view/alexbachata), [Gumroad](https://bachatabutterfly.gumroad.com/l/hakuna-bachata)
- UK in-person benchmark: City Academy London charges £110/month for unlimited salsa & bachata classes and socials. — [City Academy](https://dev.city-academy.com/salsa-bachata-membership)

### Inferences
- Learners already pay £40–£300 for content, and £110/month for in-person classes. A £2–£5/month utility for organising their *own* class recaps is a small add-on to that spend.
- Instructors are a possible B2B2C channel: they already use course platforms and white-label apps but have no tool for their students' class-recap videos.

### Gaps
- No pricing was found for Demetrio & Nicole, DanceWithDami, Ataca & Alemana, Salsa Gente, Korke & Judith's academy, Patreon dance instructors or Uscreen dance apps. Searches did not surface their official pages, so verify them directly.
- Whether these academies use Uscreen/Kajabi white-label apps was not confirmed (Passion.io is confirmed only for QueWedta).

## Note-taking / move-library apps (direct competitors to "organise your own videos")

### Takeaway
This niche is crowded with new, tiny iOS-only indie apps launched 2025–2026: Terpsi, Sweet Dance Moves Library, Dance Dex, Let's Dance, MemoDanse, DanceVault, DanceLib and Dance Cam. All have near-zero ratings, and their monetisation is cheap: lifetime $1.99–$2.99, ~$9.99/yr, or a one-time "Plus". None was found that combines position-category roadmaps, snippet cutting and automatic 8-count segmentation.

### Cited Findings
- Terpsi (released 26 Jun 2026): local/iCloud storage, trimming, tags, frame-by-frame, spaced recall, and repertoire/study-time tracking. Free core; the Plus one-time unlock adds compare and sync. <1,000 downloads. — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/terpsi-dance-practice-videos/6780994356)
- Sweet Dance Moves Library: tags by style and source, imports from social platforms, practice lists, iCloud sync. Lifetime Premium $2.99 / Yearly $9.99. 2 ratings. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6749212354)
- Dance Dex: auto-organises videos by recognised song, with custom song sections and cloud storage. Free, no IAP. 0 ratings. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6746681807)
- Let's Dance: sort by style (salsa/bachata/kizomba), event tags, filter by music/counts (search snippet; listing could not be fetched). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/be/app/lets-dance/id1456617882)
- MemoDanse: iPad, $1.99 (search snippet only). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6747616918)
- A non-app alternative also exists: a $15 Gumroad "Dance Moves Cheat Sheet" (spreadsheet of moves with video links and progress). — [Gumroad](https://bachatabutterfly.gumroad.com/l/moves)

### Inferences
- Validation: multiple independent devs have built this, so the job ("my class videos are lost in my camera roll") is real.
- Warning: willingness to pay appears low, with indie lifetime prices of $2–$3. No organiser app shows traction.
- Most competitors are iOS-only, so Spino's Android build (Play) is a possible early wedge.

### Gaps
- No download or revenue data is available for these apps beyond Terpsi's <1K estimate.
- Android equivalents were not systematically searched.

## Apps that auto-count or segment dance videos by music

### Takeaway
Spino's "upcoming" auto-8-count feature is no longer unique. DanceBeat (video beat detection, measure split, 8-count navigation, beat-grid editor) and DanceCopilot (8-count ruler on own videos) already do it on iOS, and Ritmo does an 8-count grid with phrase colouring for bachata/salsa audio and video at $4.99/mo, $49.99/yr or $149.99 lifetime. All are very new and have negligible ratings.

### Cited Findings
- DanceBeat analyses a video's extracted audio, splits beats into measures, and offers 8-count navigation, loop, slow, mirror, compare and frame-scrub. v1.0.4 added a manual beat-grid editor. It is a monthly subscription (COP 9,900 in the Colombia store). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/dancebeat/id6757979636)
- Ritmo: on-device beat analysis into an 8-count grid with phrase changes. Supports video files and YouTube links. Pro costs $4.99/mo, $49.99/yr or $149.99 lifetime. The free tier is 3 songs/10 analyses (the description says 10/20). v1.0 released 1 Oct (year not shown). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760730410)
- DanceCopilot: 8-count ruler, loop and compare on the user's own videos. 10 free imports, then a one-time purchase (search snippet; listing fetch blocked). — [chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/id6810995273)
- Dancetime – Salsa Beat Finder: live count from music, salsa only, bachata "coming soon" (snippet). — [similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/app/apple/6476049765)
- Academic precedent: ISMIR 2020 showed dance beat tracking from visual keypoints alone, and Rutgers' SalsaAsst is a mobile beat-counting system for salsa. — [ISMIR 2020](https://program.ismir2020.net/poster_3-10.html), [Rutgers SalsaAsst](https://www.winlab.rutgers.edu/~yychen/papers/SalsaAsst-%20Beat%20Counting%20System%20Empowered%20by%20Mobile%20Devices%20to%20Assist%20Salsa%20Dancers.pdf)

### Inferences
- Auto-8-count on its own is a feature, not a moat. Ritmo's pricing ($4.99/mo / $49.99/yr / $149.99 lifetime) is the most direct price anchor for a counts feature.
- **Gaps Spino could own** (none of the products found combines these):
  1. Auto-8-count *cutting* of class recap videos into reusable snippets, filed directly into a position-category roadmap.
  2. Bachata-specific structure: position vocabulary and roadmaps of positions/transitions rather than generic tags.
  3. Android plus UK focus: competitors are overwhelmingly iOS-only and US/global.
  4. An instructor channel: teachers have course platforms but nothing that helps students organise the recap videos teachers send after class.
  5. Local-first privacy (videos stay on the phone). Terpsi shares this, but most others push cloud storage.
- Pricing implication: the indie anchors are ~$2.99 lifetime to $9.99/yr (organisers) and $4.99/mo / $49.99/yr (Ritmo counts). STEEZY (£60–£100/yr) is the content ceiling. A utility-only app likely sits in the £2–£5/mo or £20–£40/yr band, and a lifetime option is common in the niche.

### Gaps
- No Android auto-count video app was found or verified.
- The accuracy of DanceBeat's and Ritmo's bachata beat detection is unknown, as are their download figures.
- I found no Reddit (r/Bachata, r/Salsa) discussion data on these tools; Reddit was not reachable within the search budget.
