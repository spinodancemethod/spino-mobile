# Bachata Partner Dance – Position Reference List

This reference organizes classic and expanded Bachata partner positions into a unified table. Each entry includes the traditional position name, a detailed description, and a structured three-part attribute system:

$$\text{[ Body Arrangement ]} \;\times\; \text{[ Partner Order ]} \;\times\; \text{[ Arm/Hand Connection ]}$$

---

| No. | Position Name | Description | Three-Part Naming Attribute |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Close Sensual Position** | Partners face each other in a close embrace Typically with hip connection. | `Facing | Equal | Closed` |
| **2** | **Open Position (Basic Position)** | Partners face each other with space between them, connected by one or both hands. | `Facing | Equal | Open` |
| **3** | **Cross-Hand Position L/R** | Partners face each other holding both hands in a crossed configuration. Leaders Left over the right | `Facing | Equal | Crossed` |
| **4** | **Cross-Hand Position R/L** | Partners face each other holding both hands in a crossed configuration. Leaders Right over the left | `Facing | Equal | Crossed` |
| **5** | **Handshake Hold LL** | Partners face each other with a single handshake-style hand connection. | `Facing | Equal | Single Hand Hold (Left-to-Left)` |
| **6** | **Handshake Hold RR** | Partners face each other with a single handshake-style hand connection. | `Facing | Equal | Single Hand Hold (Right-to-Right)` |

| **7** | **Shadow Position – Follower in Front** | Follower stands ahead, leader stands behind, both facing the same direction. | `Shadow (Same Direction) | Follower in Front | Open / Frame Contact` |
| **8** | **Reverse Shadow Position – Leader in Front** | Leader stands ahead, follower stands behind, both facing the same direction. | `Shadow (Same Direction) | Leader in Front | Open / Body Contact` |
| **9** | **Cuddle Position/ Wrap Position – Follower in Front** | Follower is wrapped by the leader, with one or both arms positioned across the body. | Side-by-Side | Follower in Front | Wrapped (Follower) |
| **10** | **Side-by-Side – Leader on Left** | Leader is alongside to the left, both facing the same direction. | `Side-by-Side | Leader Slightly Ahead | Open` |

| **11** | **Side-by-Side Leader on Right** | Follower is alongside and left of the leader, both facing the same direction. | `Side-by-Side | Follower Slightly Ahead | Open` |

| **12** | **Back-to-Back Position** | Partners stand with their backs towards each other. | `Back-to-Back | Equal | Open / Touch` |
| **13** | **Hammerlock – Leader Left arm** | Leader's left arm is guided behind their back. | `Facing / Side-by-Side | Equal | Hammerlock (Leader)` |
| **14** | **Hammerlock – Leader Right arm** | Leader's right arm is guided behind their back. | `Facing / Side-by-Side | Equal | Hammerlock (Leader)` |
| **15** | **Hammerlock – Follower left arm** | Follower's left arm is guided behind their back. | `Facing / Side-by-Side | Equal | Hammerlock (Follower)` |
| **16** | **Hammerlock – Follower right arm** | Follower's right arm is guided behind their back. | `Facing / Side-by-Side | Equal | Hammerlock (Follower)` |

---

### Three-Part Taxonomy Summary

Using this standard schema allows for clear categorizations when expanding your curriculum or documenting new transitions:

1. **Body Arrangement:** `Facing`, `Shadow (Same Direction)`, `Side-by-Side`, `Promenade`, `Back-to-Back`, `Opposite`
2. **Partner Order:** `Equal`, `Leader in Front`, `Follower in Front`, `Leader Left`, `Leader Right`
3. **Arm / Hand Connection:** `Closed`, `Open`, `Crossed`, `Single Hand Hold`, `Wrapped`, `Hammerlock`
